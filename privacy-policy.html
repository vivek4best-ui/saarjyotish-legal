3. What We Collect

3.1 Account identity

If you create an account: your mobile number and/or email address, its verification status, account creation timestamp, and (only if you separately, explicitly opt in via its own un-pre-ticked checkbox) marketing-consent status/version/timestamp. We never send a real OTP code to your device from our servers in production without your request for it — the OTP itself is never logged in a human-readable way beyond a short-lived hashed comparison, and expires automatically.

3.2 Birth-chart / Kundali data

Stored only on your own device. When you request a result, it is sent to our server for that single calculation and is not saved there (verified: the calculation endpoints do not write it to any database table).

3.3 Your questions and AI Guruji conversation content

Sent to our server to generate/relate to an answer. The server tracks only a message count against your paid unlock — not the message text itself, and not your identity, unless a future live-AI-provider integration changes this (Section 7.3 will be updated first, before that feature goes live).

3.4 Transaction/payment and wallet information

Fee type, amount, status, the question it relates to, and (if you were logged in at purchase time) a link to your account so the purchase is recoverable if you sign in on a new device. Wallet activity is a full, auditable transaction ledger (credits/debits/ refunds), never a bare mutable balance.

3.5 Device/session/security information

For admin/Jyotishi-tier users: a login-session token and 2FA state. For end-user accounts: a revocable session token. For crash reporting: an error message, stack trace, platform, app version, and an opaque per-install session id — never linked to any device identifier or personal data.

3.6 Consent records

Marketing consent (Section 3.1) and Terms/Privacy acceptance are each their own explicit, timestamped, versioned record — never implied by account creation.

4. Purposes of Processing

Account identity: to authenticate you and let you recover purchases/wallet balance across devices. Birth-chart data: solely to compute the result you requested, in that single request. Payment/ wallet data: to grant, verify, and let you review your entitlements. Crash reports: to fix bugs.

5. Legal Basis / Consent

Account creation and OTP verification are based on your consent (by using the login feature) and legitimate necessity to provide the service you requested. Marketing communications are based solely on your separate, explicit, revocable consent (Section 6).

6. Consent Withdrawal

You may withdraw marketing consent at any time from within the app (the same toggle used to grant it); this takes effect immediately and is recorded with its own timestamp/version.

7. Service Providers / Processors

7.1 Hosting infrastructure: our backend and database are hosted on Railway (Railway Corporation), currently provisioned in Railway's US (San Francisco) region (astrologyapp-server-production.up.railway.app). Railway acts as our data processor for whatever the backend stores (see Section 3) -- it does not independently use your data for its own purposes.

7.2 OTP/SMS/email delivery: not yet integrated — no real vendor receives your mobile number/email for OTP delivery today (per an owner decision, SMS/OTP vendor selection is not required for the current product scope); this section will name the actual vendor and their data-handling terms if that changes.

7.3 Payment processor: not yet integrated — payments are processed via a non-production test/mock flow only.

7.4 AI provider: not yet integrated — the AI Guruji chat feature is not powered by any live external AI provider; this section will name the provider and disclose their training/ retention terms before that feature goes live, not after.

8. Security

Server-side OTP hashing (never stored/logged in plain text), rate-limiting on authentication/OTP endpoints, revocable session tokens with expiry, two-factor authentication for admin/Jyotishi accounts, CORS restrictions, and fail-closed production startup checks. We do not claim any specific encryption-at-rest mechanism unless independently verified as actually implemented.

9. Retention and Deletion

  • Birth-chart/Kundali data: on your device only, until you delete it.
  • Account identity (mobile/email/verification/consent): retained while your account is active; irreversibly cleared upon account deletion (Section 12) — not merely hidden.
  • Transaction/wallet records: retained for accounting/dispute- resolution/legal-compliance purposes for the period required by applicable law, but no longer linked to your identity once your account is deleted.
  • Crash reports: a reasonable debugging window; never linked to your identity.

10. Your Rights

Once you have an account, you can, from within the app: view your account/wallet/purchase data (Section 3), withdraw marketing consent (Section 6), and delete your account (Section 12), which is the functional equivalent of an erasure request for everything we hold that identifies you. You can also delete your account from this website without opening the app — see Account Deletion. For any other access/correction request, contact us (Section 2).

11. Account Deletion

A genuine deletion mechanism exists both in-app and on the web (account-deletion.html, same page Google Play's Data Safety section links to): your mobile number/email are permanently removed and every verification/consent flag cleared; transaction records necessary for legal/accounting retention remain but no longer identify you.

12. Grievance Process

Contact us via Section 2; we acknowledge and respond within a reasonable time consistent with applicable law.

13. International/Cross-Border Processing

Your data is processed and stored on servers located in the United States (Railway's San Francisco region, per Section 7.1), even though you may be using the App from India or elsewhere. By using the App, you acknowledge this cross-border transfer.

14. Cookies/Web Technologies

This page and account-deletion.html use no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts — they only call our own API when you actively submit a form. The mobile App's own data practices are covered above; this note only concerns these specific web pages.

15. Children/Minors

The App does not knowingly target children. Users must be 18 years of age or older, enforced via a self-declaration checkbox at the point of payment (recorded server-side with a timestamp) — not an independently verified age-check.
